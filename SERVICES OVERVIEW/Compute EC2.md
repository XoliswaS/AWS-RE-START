# Lab Report: Mastering Amazon EC2 (Elastic Compute Cloud)

## ☁️ Overview
**Amazon EC2** provides scalable, on-demand virtual servers in the AWS cloud. During this lab, I practiced how to provision, manage, and configure cloud instances, eliminating the need for physical hardware and enabling a flexible environment for hosting websites and backend services.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Competencies Gained

### **1. Instance Provisioning & Configuration**
* **Launch Mechanics:** I learned the step-by-step process of launching EC2 instances tailored to specific workload requirements.
* **AMI Selection:** I gained an understanding of **Amazon Machine Images (AMIs)**—selecting the right OS and software stack for the environment.
* **Instance Types:** I explored how to choose instance types based on CPU, memory, and networking needs (e.g., T-series for burstable performance).

### **2. Security & Connectivity**
* **Secure Access:** I utilized **Key Pairs (SSH keys)** to establish encrypted connections to my instances.
* **Firewall Management:** I configured **Security Groups** to act as a virtual firewall, controlling inbound and outbound traffic at the instance level.

### **3. Operational Management**
* **Linux Administration:** I practiced fundamental Linux CLI commands to navigate and manage the instance once connected.
* **Lifecycle Management:** I mastered the differences between **Starting**, **Stopping**, and **Terminating** instances—understanding the implications for both data persistence and billing.

---

## 🚀 Why EC2 Matters
Amazon EC2 is the backbone of cloud architecture. It offers:
* **Agility:** Deploy servers in minutes rather than weeks.
* **Scalability:** Effortlessly scale compute power up or down based on demand.
* **Cost-Efficiency:** The pay-as-you-go model ensures resources are only paid for when they are running.

---

## 📝 Final Reflection
This lab reinforced the importance of "Infrastructure as Code" concepts and gave me the hands-on experience needed to deploy reliable, secure, and scalable applications in a cloud-native environment.
